# U.S. Coast Guard — Overview for Prospective Recruits

The Coast Guard is a military service and federal law enforcement agency under the Department of Homeland Security (can operate under Navy control during wartime).

## Mission areas

Search and rescue, maritime law enforcement, port security, environmental protection, aids to navigation, and ice operations define Coast Guard missions. Members serve on cutters, at air stations, and at sector commands nationwide.

## Training

Coast Guard Recruit Training at Cape May, New Jersey lasts 8 weeks. Follow-on training assigns ratings such as Boatswain's Mate, Machinery Technician, Aviation Survival Technician, or Intelligence Specialist.

## Dual role

Coast Guard members enforce federal law and perform defense operations. Law enforcement authority distinguishes the service from other branches in daily operations.

## Enlistment

The Coast Guard is selective with limited accession slots compared to larger branches. Strong ASVAB scores, clean background, and medical qualification improve competitiveness.

## Benefits

Coast Guard members receive military pay, TRICARE, GI Bill eligibility, and retirement benefits similar to other services.

## Next steps

Work with a Coast Guard recruiter early — wait times can be longer than other branches during competitive periods.
