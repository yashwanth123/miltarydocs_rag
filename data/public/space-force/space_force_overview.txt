# U.S. Space Force — Overview for Prospective Recruits

Established in 2019, the Space Force organizes, trains, and equips forces to conduct space operations supporting U.S. national security and allied operations.

## Mission focus

Satellite operations, missile warning, space domain awareness, cyber operations tied to space systems, and acquisition of space capabilities are core mission areas.

## Accession pathways

Many Space Force members transfer from the Air Force. Direct enlistment opportunities expand over time — recruiters explain current accession models and specialty codes (SFSCs aligned with Air Force AFSC structures).

## Career fields

Space operations, intelligence, cyber, engineering, and acquisition roles support orbital and ground-based systems. Technical aptitude and security clearance eligibility are important factors.

## Training

Initial training follows Air Force accession pipelines for many members, with Space Force-specific indoctrination on space doctrine and organizational culture.

## Why join

The Space Force offers cutting-edge technical missions with growing civilian credential relevance in aerospace and cyber sectors.

## Next steps

Contact a Space Force or Air Force recruiter for current direct-enlist and transfer options, clearance requirements, and technical aptitude thresholds.
