# U.S. Air Force — Overview for Prospective Recruits

The Air Force organizes, trains, and equips forces for air superiority, global strike, mobility, space operations (alongside Space Force missions), cyberspace operations, and intelligence.

## Basic Military Training (BMT)

Air Force BMT at Joint Base San Antonio-Lackland, Texas lasts approximately 8.5 weeks. Training emphasizes discipline, fitness, weapons familiarization, and Air Force core values.

## Air Force Specialty Codes (AFSC)

Enlisted careers span maintenance, cyber, intelligence, medical, security forces, logistics, and more. Technical fields often require strong ASVAB mechanical and electronics line scores.

## Quality of life

The Air Force often emphasizes technical training and base quality-of-life programs. Assignment locations vary worldwide from major installations to deployed environments.

## Enlistment and education

The Air Force offers college tuition assistance, CCAF degree programs, and credentialing opportunities. Height/weight and medical standards apply at MEPS.

## Space Force note

Some members may serve in U.S. Space Force units while maintaining Air Force accession pathways — ask recruiters about current transfer and direct-enlist options.

## Next steps

Contact an Air Force recruiter, prepare for the ASVAB, and review AFSC availability which changes with force structure needs.
