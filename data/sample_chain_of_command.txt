Military Chain of Command Overview

UNCLASSIFIED

The military chain of command is the line of authority and responsibility through which orders are transmitted. Each service member reports to a superior, and commanders are responsible for the welfare, training, and mission execution of subordinates.

Example chain of command (U.S. Army):
1. President of the United States
2. Secretary of Defense
3. Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff
4. Chief of Staff of the Army
5. Commanding General, FORSCOM
6. Division Commander
7. Brigade Commander
8. Battalion Commander
9. Company Commander
10. Platoon Leader

Key principles:
- Unity of command: every soldier receives orders from one superior.
- Span of control: leaders should manage a manageable number of subordinates.
- Delegation of authority: commanders may delegate authority but not responsibility.
